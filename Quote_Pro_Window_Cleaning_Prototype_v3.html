Estimate display

When enabled, customers can see itemised prices alongside the total in the live estimate and final quote summary. When off, only the total estimate appears; individual window and extra prices remain hidden. Window selection cards never show prices.

Estimate buffer

An estimate buffer creates a natural minimum–maximum price range to allow for misunderstandings when customers count or describe their windows. For example, a £25 estimate with a 10% buffer becomes £22–£28. The lower end is rounded down to the nearest whole pound and the upper end rounded up, so customers never see pennies in the buffered estimate. It is not an extra charge; it communicates an estimated range, with the final price subject to confirmation. Existing item price ranges are included first, then the buffer is applied to the total.

For example, ±10% or ±£10. The minimum charge remains enforced on the lower estimate.

Cleaning frequencies and minimum charge

Choose which cleaning schedules customers can book. Tap + Add frequency to enter any interval, such as “Every 6 weeks”, “Every 2 months” or “One-off clean”. Edit a name directly, untick Show to hide it without losing it, or tap Delete to remove it. You can add as many as you need. Optionally tick Increase price for this frequency and enter a percentage (for example, 15%) for each interval; leaving it off uses the normal rates. Keep at least one option enabled, then tap Save changes at the bottom. Only enabled choices appear on page 2 of the customer calculator.
