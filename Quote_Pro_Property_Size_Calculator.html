Estimate settings

A buffer creates a natural range for possible differences in the work described. For example, £25 ±10% is displayed as £22–£28 (rounded outward to whole pounds). It is not an additional charge. Your minimum charge is respected.

When off, customers see only the total estimate, never individual prices. Property and extra selection buttons never show prices.
